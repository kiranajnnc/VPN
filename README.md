                                            ## Answers to Interview Questions

1. What is a VPN?
   - A VPN (Virtual Private Network) is a service that creates a secure, encrypted connection between your device and a remote server, masking your real IP address and allowing you to browse the internet privately.

2. How does a VPN protect privacy?
   - A VPN protects privacy by hiding your actual IP address, encrypting your internet traffic, and routing it through a remote server so third parties—including ISPs and hackers—cannot easily track or intercept your online activity.

3. What is tunneling in VPNs?
   - Tunneling is the process of encapsulating your data within a “tunnel,” allowing it to travel securely and privately between your device and the VPN server.

4. What is the role of encryption?
   - Encryption scrambles your internet data so only the intended recipient (VPN server) can read it. This ensures data cannot be understood by anyone intercepting it during transmission.

5. What are common VPN protocols?
   - Common VPN protocols include OpenVPN, WireGuard, IKEv2/IPSec, and L2TP/IPSec. These protocols define how the encrypted data travels between your device and the server.

6. How do VPNs affect network speed?
   - VPNs can reduce network speed because your data is routed through additional servers and extra encryption processes, especially if those servers are far away or busy.
